YouTube Tidy

Mobile YouTube with no Shorts and less jank, on your iPhone home screen.

  1. Get the Userscripts app

    Free on the App Store. It's a Safari extension that runs small scripts on websites.

    Open App Store
  2. Turn it on in Safari

    In Safari, tap the page menu in the address bar → Manage Extensions → turn on Userscripts. Then tap Userscripts in that menu and allow it on youtube.com.

  3. Install YouTube Tidy

    Open this link in Safari, then tap Userscripts in the page menu and choose Install.

    Install script

    Didn't work? Copy it, open the Userscripts app, tap + → New JS, paste and save.

  4. Put it on your home screen

    Go to m.youtube.com in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Switch Open as Web App OFF, otherwise Safari extensions don't run and you'd get plain YouTube.

What it changes

  • Shorts gone: shelves, the Shorts tab, the Shorts filter, and Shorts in search.
  • Any Shorts link you open plays as a normal video with a seek bar.
  • No "Open the app" or "Try Premium" pop-ups.
  • Tapping search no longer zooms the page.
  • No double-tap zoom or tap delay on buttons.
  • Less bounce and feed jumping while thumbnails load.
