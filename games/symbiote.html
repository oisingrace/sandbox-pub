    SYMBIOTE

    A host-hopping stealth game

    You are a slime that lives inside people. Launch yourself out of one body and into the next. Hit someone and they're yours. Miss, and you're a puddle on the floor, and everyone wants to stamp on you. The body you leave behind dies too.

    Controls
    LookArrow keys ←→↑↓, or the mouse (click to capture it) MoveWASD, Shift to run LaunchHold left mouse to see the arc, let go to jump. Aim at a person. Use itemRight mouse or F: pistol, sledgehammer or charges. Keycards work by themselves. Swap itemQ when your host holds more than one ZoomZ with a sniper rifle ConsumeHold C where nobody can see you, on levels that allow it: eat your host and your slime grows a form. Skill treeEat hosts (hold C, unseen) to earn skill points, and spend them with T: Slime (hop, long launch, thick skin), Stealth (puddle, quick eater, shrink, ghost) and Strength (grow a body, brute, giant). Skills reset each level. MutationsAfter Blackwater Lab: X speed boost, K self destruct (blows up your host, costs 3 eaten, once a level). After the State House: H disguise as a prop. After the Grand Museum: L lure, J daze. BodiesHold B as a slime to grow a body (3+ eaten: brute, 6: giant), or V to shrink. Both use up everyone you've eaten since the last one. Big bodiesF smashes walls, doors and people, G picks things up and throws them. Everyone panics on sight, they can't use keycards or lifts, and a giant won't fit indoors. OtherR restart, P or Esc pause TouchLeft stick moves, drag the right side to look, hold LAUNCH to aim and let go to jump. ITEM uses what your host is holding, SWAP changes item, hold EAT to consume your host where the level allows it, hold BODY to grow a body.
