    SYMBIOTE

    A host-hopping stealth game

    You are a slime that lives inside people. Launch yourself out of one body and into the next. Hit someone and they're yours. Miss, and you're a puddle on the floor, and everyone wants to stamp on you. The body you leave behind dies too.

    Hollow Hill
    • Stealth Get inside the compound without setting off a lockdown.
    • Espionage Download the files in the server room.
    • Assassination Take out the Director.
    • Destruction Bring down the relay tower.
    • Extraction Escape down the storm drain.
